## 🛠️ CAD Foundations: Autodesk Fusion 360

- **Geometric Primitives**: Creating lines, rectangles, and foundational sketches.
- **Parametric Operations**: Extruding 2D sketches into 3D solid bodies, mirroring symmetrical elements, and applying fill/fillet operations.
- **Design Thinking Integration**: Learning how simple digital tools gain immense power when combined with systematic design thinking.

## 🖐️ Hands-On Exploration: Clay Modelling

The afternoon took us away from computer screens into tactile, hands-on creativity with clay and dough modelling:

### My First Step into 3D Printing & Slicing Optimization (2026-09-29)

# My First Step into 3D Printing & Slicing Optimization

> *Innovation is not just about having a creative idea—it is also about understanding how to turn that idea into a physical object.*

---

## 🎯 Focus Areas & Hardware
- **Bambu Studio**: Advanced Slicing, Layer Height, Infill Density, Support Structures
- **Bambu Lab H2S**: High-speed precision 3D printer at Forge
- **Manufacturing Constraints**: Weight target `< 50g`, Print duration `~1h 30m`
- **Engineering Iteration**: Idea → Digital Model → Fabrication Setup → Machine → Physical Object

---

## 🖨️ Slicing & Preparation in Bambu Studio

We installed and configured **Bambu Studio** to prepare a functional phone holder model for the **Bambu Lab H2S** 3D printer:

- **Model Orientation**: Experimenting with build plate orientation to minimize print time and material usage while ensuring structural integrity.
- **Slicing Mechanics**: Converting 3D surface meshes into G-code layers and previewing nozzle toolpaths.
- **Optimization Challenges**: Tuning print parameters to meet strict production benchmarks:
  - **Weight constraint**: Keep total filament usage below **50 grams**.
  - **Time constraint**: Complete the print in approximately **1 hour 30 minutes**.
- **Engineering Trade-offs**: Evaluating how minor adjustments in layer height, infill patterns, and support placements impact durability, finish, and print speed.

![Bambu Lab H2S 3D Printer with AMS 2 and Printed Phone Holder Parts](/assets/weekly/Week_06/02_Tuesday/bambu_h2s_print_plate.jpg)

---

## 🔄 The Complete Engineering Journey

This sprint transformed how I view digital engineering. What once seemed like isolated geometry on a screen became a full manufacturing pipeline:

1. **Design**: Sketching and parametric modeling in Autodesk Fusion 360
2. **Test & Simulate**: Checking tolerances, toolpaths, and slice configurations
3. **Adjust**: Refining orientation and slicing parameters in Bambu Studio
4. **Manufacture**: Fabricating on laser cutters (RDWorks V8) and 3D printers (Bambu Lab H2S)
5. **Discover & Improve**: Real engineering starts when physical feedback reveals unexpected improvements.
