# From 3D Models to Laser-Cut Parts

> *A model on the screen is only the beginning. It has to be created in the right format, prepared correctly, and translated into instructions that a machine can understand.*

---

## 🎯 Focus Areas & Tools
- **Digital Fabrication**: Laser Cutting Fundamentals
- **RDWorks V8**: Laser cutting preparation software & vector toolpaths
- **File Interoperability**: DXF file generation and machine translation

---

## ⚙️ Laser Cutting Preparation & Workflow

We advanced into digital manufacturing by bridging CAD models with laser fabrication machines:

- **Fundamentals of Laser Cutting**: Understanding laser power, speed, kerf width, and material considerations.
- **Workflow in RDWorks V8**: Preparing 2D profiles for laser cutting and importing formatted DXF files into RDWorks V8.
- **Design-to-Manufacturing Bridge**: Realizing that digital geometry must adhere to strict machine constraints to be manufacturable.

---

## 💡 Key Shift: "I Designed It" → "I Can Manufacture It"

That shift—from simply designing an object to understanding how a physical machine processes and cuts it—was a pivotal milestone.

Translating digital designs into precise machine instructions connects design theory with real-world fabrication.
