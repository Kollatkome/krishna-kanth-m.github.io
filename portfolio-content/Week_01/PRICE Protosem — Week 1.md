17–21 August 2026

## Week 1 Overview

The first week of PRICE Protosem was about moving beyond a traditional classroom experience and developing the mindset needed for entrepreneurship. Across five days, we focused on connecting with others, understanding ourselves, working together, taking ownership, and learning from real entrepreneurial experiences.

The week progressed from **connection → self-discovery → collaboration → ownership → entrepreneurship**.

---

## Day 1 — Break the Ice and Build Connections

**17 August 2026**

The first day began with a general introduction to the PRICE Protosem programme and an opportunity to get to know the people around us.

We participated in icebreaker activities, including **Rock Paper Scissors**, which helped everyone interact, communicate, compete and become more comfortable with one another.

### What we experienced

- Introduction to the PRICE Protosem programme
    
- Getting to know each other
    
- Icebreaking activities
    
- Communication and interaction
    
- Building initial connections
    

### What we learned

Before working together to develop ideas, it is important to establish communication and connection. The first day helped create a comfortable environment for interaction and collaboration.

**Key takeaway: CONNECT**

---

## Day 2 — Know Yourself and Your Journey

**18 August 2026**

The second day focused on understanding ourselves and the way we think.

We explored **16 Personalities**, which helped us reflect on our strengths, preferences and different thinking styles. We also experienced **Zen Pencils**, which encouraged us to think about our own journey and perspective.

### What we explored

- Personal strengths
    
- Individual preferences
    
- Thinking styles
    
- Self-awareness
    
- Personal journeys and perspectives
    

### What we learned

Everyone approaches situations differently. Understanding our own strengths and thinking patterns can help us work better with others and make better decisions.

**Key takeaway: UNDERSTAND YOURSELF**

---

## Day 3 — Trust and Teamwork

**19 August 2026**

The third day focused strongly on collaboration, communication and teamwork through practical challenges.

We experienced **Among Us — Real Life**, where observation, communication, trust and decision-making were essential. We also participated in the **Marshmallow Challenge**, where we had to listen, build, experiment and adapt while working toward a common goal.

### What we practiced

- Observation
    
- Communication
    
- Trust
    
- Decision-making
    
- Listening
    
- Building
    
- Experimentation
    
- Adaptation
    

### What we learned

Teamwork is not simply about dividing tasks. Effective collaboration requires listening to others, communicating clearly, trusting the process, experimenting with ideas and adapting when something does not work.

**Key takeaway: TRUST + TEAMWORK**

---

## Day 4 — From Participants to Pioneers

**20 August 2026**

The fourth day marked a transition from simply participating in the programme to taking ownership of the journey.

The day included an orientation session covering the **7 FORGE principles**, with emphasis on mindset, discipline and growth. We were also introduced to the **scoreboard**, which connected our progress with personal ownership.

The **PRICE First Batch Inauguration** provided another important experience. Through the session with **Rai Kumar, CEO of RAI**, we were encouraged to look at retail from the customer's point of view.

### What we explored

- Mindset
    
- Discipline
    
- Growth
    
- Personal progress
    
- Ownership
    
- Customer perspective
    
- Asking questions
    
- Looking at problems from different perspectives
    
- Developing solutions
    

### What we learned

Understanding a problem requires more than simply looking at it from our own perspective. We need to observe, ask questions and understand the experience of the customer before thinking about solutions.

**Key takeaway: OBSERVE • QUESTION • OWN**

---

## Day 5 — Entrepreneurship Beyond Theory

**21 August 2026**

The fifth day connected the week's learning to real entrepreneurial experience.

We attended the **YEP Kick-Off — World Entrepreneurs' Day at KCT**, featuring **Ramkrishna Muthu, Managing Director of Thulasi Pharmacies**.

His real-life experiences highlighted the journey of entrepreneurship through:

**Challenge → Choice → Courage → Consistency → Growth**

The session showed that entrepreneurship is not created through one major event. Growth comes from the choices and actions made consistently over time.

### What we explored

- Facing challenges
    
- Making choices
    
- Having courage
    
- Staying consistent
    
- Continuous growth
    
- Learning from real entrepreneurial experiences
    

### What we learned

Entrepreneurship is a continuous process. Challenges require decisions, decisions require courage, and long-term progress requires consistency.

**Key takeaway: EXPERIENCE • PERSIST • CREATE**

---

# What We Learned Throughout Week 1

The five days gradually introduced a different part of the entrepreneurial mindset.

### 1. Connect

We started by interacting with others and building relationships.

### 2. Discover

We reflected on our own personality, strengths and ways of thinking.

### 3. Collaborate

We learned that communication, trust, listening and adaptation are essential when working together.

### 4. Own

We began taking greater responsibility for our progress and learned to look at problems from the customer's perspective.

### 5. Create

We connected these experiences with entrepreneurship and understood the importance of persistence, courage and continuous action.
