# Week 1 — Technology, Design Thinking, and Retail

The first structured week focused on understanding technology, design thinking, and the retail industry. The emphasis was on looking at real-world business challenges and understanding how technology and creative problem-solving can be used to develop meaningful solutions.

The week began with discussions about recommendation systems and how data-driven technologies can influence digital experiences. Design thinking sessions introduced user-centric approaches to solving problems, emphasizing the importance of understanding users, their needs, and the context surrounding a problem.

Industry analysis formed another major part of the week. The food and beverage retail sector was explored through concepts such as value chains and SWOT analysis. This helped identify different stages of a business process, understand stakeholders, recognize opportunities, and examine challenges within an industry.

The sessions also introduced decision-making and consumer behavior. Understanding how people make decisions provided a connection between technology, business, and user needs. Alongside this, work on professional portfolios highlighted the importance of documenting skills, projects, and achievements effectively.

The later sessions focused on inspiration, leadership, entrepreneurship, and personal development. Discussions around goals, vision, career growth, innovation, and entrepreneurship provided a broader understanding of how technical skills can be combined with business awareness and creative thinking.

By the end of Week 1, the learning had moved beyond technology alone. The week established a foundation involving **technology, business understanding, design thinking, consumer behavior, communication, and personal development**.
